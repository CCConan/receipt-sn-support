Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 18, 2026 | Version: v3.1

Thank you for choosing Receipt SN. Receipt SN is operated by Studio Nestir Limited ("Company", "Data Controller"). This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data. Please read carefully before using the app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Receipt Data: Receipt images you capture or import, along with extracted information such as merchant name, amount, date, and line items.
  • Device Information: Basic details like device model, OS version, and app version for diagnostics.
  • Error Logs: Anonymous error reports collected when the app encounters issues to help improve stability.
  • Subscription Information: Your subscription status is managed by Apple App Store. We do not store your complete payment information.

2. Lawful Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: To provide the receipt scanning and digitization services.
  • Explicit Consent: For optional features like iCloud synchronization.
  • Legal Obligations: For maintaining financial records related to subscriptions.

3. How We Use Your Information

Receipt SN uses your data to:

  • Provide AI recognition features to extract structured data from receipts
  • Organize and categorize your data
  • Generate statistical reports and export files
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues

4. AI and Automated Processing

Receipt SN will never use your receipt images, text, or recognition results to train AI models. Your data is used solely to provide the services you request.

When cloud-based AI services are needed for recognition, data is processed transiently and is not retained for training purposes.

5. Sub-processors (Third-Party Processing)

To provide high-quality AI features, we utilize the following sub-processors:

  • Google Cloud Platform (Gemini API): Processes transient text/images for categorization and extraction via encrypted transit.
  • Apple Inc.: Handles document synchronization (iCloud) and payment processing (App Store).

6. California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected.
  • Delete personal information collected from you.
  • Opt-out of the "Sale" of personal info (Note: We do NOT sell user data).

7. Data Storage

Receipt SN stores your data on your local device by default. If you enable iCloud, data is stored using Apple's infrastructure. Deleting the app removes all local data.

8. Data Security

We use encryption to protect your data, including local storage encryption and TLS/SSL for transmission. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

You can export your data anytime, delete individual receipts, or remove the app to delete all local data.

10. Children's Privacy

Receipt SN is rated 4+ and is suitable for users aged 4 and older. Due to our local-first privacy architecture, we do not collect personal data from any users, including children.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Receipt SN after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
