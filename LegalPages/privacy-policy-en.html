Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 25, 2026 | Version: v3.4

Thank you for choosing Receipt SN. Receipt SN is operated by Studio Nestir Limited ("Studio Nestir", "we", "us", or "our"). Where applicable data protection law uses the concept of a data controller, Studio Nestir is the controller for personal data processed under this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Receipt SN is designed as a personal receipt organization tool. We aim to keep your receipt records under your control, use service providers only where needed to deliver requested features, and describe our practices in a clear and practical way.

1. Information Processed by the App (Stored Locally by Default)

Depending on how you use Receipt SN, the following categories of information may be processed. By default, receipt records are stored on your device and are not synced to iCloud unless you enable iCloud Sync. We do not use our servers as a permanent receipt storage service, and we do not sell your receipt data or use it for advertising, data brokerage, or advertising profiles.

  • Receipt content: receipt images you take or import, and structured information extracted from them, such as merchant names, dates, totals, taxes, payment method labels, categories, notes, line items, and other information you choose to scan, import, edit, or store. If you use Cloud AI recognition, receipt images or text may be processed by our selected service providers. We do not store users' raw data on our servers.
  • User-created content: spaces, labels, category selections, export preferences, and other records you create inside the app.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, region, and basic technical information needed for compatibility and troubleshooting.
  • Diagnostics and support information: crash information, error logs, and messages you send to us when requesting help. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.
  • Subscription information: subscription status, product identifiers, renewal state, and related App Store purchase metadata. Apple processes payment details; we do not receive your full card number or Apple ID password.

2. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you, when you scan receipts, edit records, export files, contact support, or change settings.
  • From your device, when the app performs OCR, stores local data, checks subscription status, or records technical diagnostics.
  • From Apple, for App Store purchases, subscription validation, iCloud sync, TestFlight distribution, and other Apple platform services.
  • From service providers, where a feature you request requires cloud processing, routing, diagnostics, or customer support.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only for reasonable product, support, security, and legal purposes, including to:

  • scan receipts and extract structured data such as merchant, date, total, category, and line items;
  • organize, search, display, edit, export, and back up receipt records according to your settings;
  • verify subscription status, apply usage limits, and provide paid features;
  • maintain app security, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and improve reliability;
  • respond to support requests, legal requests, and obligations that apply to us.

4. AI and Automated Processing

We do not sell your receipt data, and we do not use it for advertising, data brokerage, building advertising profiles, or training our own general AI models.

Receipt SN uses automated recognition to help convert receipt images into editable records. AI and OCR results may be incomplete, duplicated, misread, or incorrect. You remain responsible for reviewing and correcting records before relying on them for tax, accounting, reimbursement, legal, or financial purposes.

When you choose or use a feature that requires cloud-based recognition, the receipt image and related text may be processed by our backend and/or selected AI service providers. We do not store users' raw data. We design this processing solely to provide the requested feature, not to create public datasets or train generalized models from your receipt content.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers to operate Receipt SN. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to us or to you.

  • Apple Inc.: App Store purchases, subscription management, TestFlight distribution, iCloud synchronization, device-level privacy controls, and platform services.
  • Cloud AI and infrastructure providers: receipt recognition, routing, OCR/AI extraction, hosting, security, and service reliability. Receipt content may be processed temporarily when cloud recognition is used.
  • Support and diagnostics providers: crash reporting, technical diagnostics, and customer support where enabled or required to resolve issues.

We do not authorize service providers to sell your receipt data. Some providers may process data in countries different from where you live, subject to their security and data processing commitments.

6. Local Storage, iCloud, and Retention

Receipt SN stores receipt records primarily on your device. If you delete individual records, they are removed from the app's local database subject to normal device and backup behavior.

iCloud synchronization: iCloud Sync is optional. If you enable it, receipt records may sync through Apple's iCloud infrastructure between devices signed in to your Apple Account. We do not host your private iCloud database and do not have direct access to records stored only in your private iCloud account.

Retention: Local records remain until you delete them, reset the app, or remove the app from your device. Cloud processing requests are intended to be transient unless temporary retention is needed for security, debugging, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or service operation. Support emails and related correspondence may be retained as long as reasonably necessary to handle your request and maintain business records.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Where laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, our legal bases may include:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide receipt scanning, storage, subscriptions, exports, and related app features.
  • Consent: for optional features such as cloud recognition, iCloud sync, diagnostics, or permissions where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the app, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, improve reliability, and respond to user requests.
  • Legal obligations: to comply with applicable tax, accounting, consumer, platform, and legal requirements.

8. International Transfers

Receipt SN may be used in different countries, and our service providers may process information in jurisdictions with privacy laws that differ from your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, contractual commitments, platform controls, or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including encrypted network transmission where data is sent to cloud services and security controls provided by Apple and our infrastructure providers. No app, device, or network can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You can review, edit, export, and delete receipt records in the app. You can disable optional features such as cloud recognition or iCloud sync where the app provides those controls. You can also uninstall the app to remove local app data from your device, subject to any iCloud, device backup, or exported copies you maintain.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent. California residents may also request information about personal information practices and deletion rights under applicable California privacy laws. We do not sell personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

The app may be rated for general audiences by the App Store, but receipt management, subscription, and cloud recognition features are intended to be used by adults or under appropriate parental or guardian supervision. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information for behavioral advertising or sale.
